# SWYNEX – Exploratory Data Analysis

## Project Overview
This project performs Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) on a cleaned Superstore sales dataset to understand sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product categories, and discount patterns.

## Objectives
- Analyze sales and profit by region.
- Compare sales across product categories and sub-categories.
- Evaluate customer segment performance.
- Identify loss-making products.
- Examine the relationship between discounts and profitability.

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas and NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## Project Workflow
1. Load the cleaned dataset.
2. Inspect the dataset structure and summary statistics.
3. Check missing values and duplicate records.
4. Perform exploratory data analysis.
5. Create charts to visualize business trends.
6. Document findings and recommendations.

## Key Findings
1. The West region has the highest total sales among the regions analyzed.
2. Technology has the highest sales among the three product categories.
3. Tables, Bookcases, and Supplies have negative total profits.
4. Transactions with very high discounts can have negative profits.
5. The Consumer segment has the highest sales and profit among the customer segments analyzed.

## Recommendations
- Review pricing and costs for loss-making products.
- Evaluate discount policies to protect profit margins.
- Monitor sales and profitability together.
- Maintain strong performance in profitable regions and product categories.
